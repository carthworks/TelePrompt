Good morning, everyone. Thank you for taking the time to be here today.

Most assessment tools treat every question as equal. An easy question and an expert-level question shouldn't have the same meaning when we're evaluating someone's ability.

So the system looks at the difficulty of the question, whether the learner answered correctly, and even the possibility of guessing. This helps us understand the learner's actual ability rather than simply saying, "You got 70 percent, so your ability is 70 percent."

The second concept is Subject-as-Data. We don't want to build one assessment system for Python, another for medicine, another for law, and another for system design.

Instead, the assessment engine stays the same, and the subject becomes the data that we give to the engine. So if tomorrow we want to support a new domain, we add content — not code.

That is what lets us scale across subjects, institutions and learners without rebuilding the core every time.
